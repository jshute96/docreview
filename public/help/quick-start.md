Welcome to Docreview! This tool helps manage your inbox of Google Docs under review.

## Get started: Add documents

One of three ways:

1. **Click Refresh** to sync updated documents from Google Drive and Gmail.<br>The first refresh adds documents updated in the last 7 days.

2. **Click Add** to add a specific document.

3. **Click Load** to selectively backfill older documents from Drive or Gmail.

## The documents view

This shows documents in your "Inbox" by default: Documents with recent comments to look at.

* Click **Archive** to remove a document from the inbox.
* Click **Open** to open the document.
* Click **Edit** to add labels or notes.
* You can **Filter** by status or label.<br>Filter buttons cycle inclusion vs exclusion.

Documents with items mentioning you or assigned to you are highlighted.

## The comments view

**Click a document title** to open Docreview's comments view.

* The default view shows comments in inbox state.
  * On your documents, all new comments get added to your Inbox.
  * On other documents, replies to your comments get added.
* Click **Archive** to hide a comment.
  * Click **Mute** to permanently hide a comment.  Replies won't enter your inbox.
* Click a comment thread to open it inline.  You can **Reply** or **Resolve** here.
* Comments in green are marked read.  The documents view shows the **Unread** comment count.

## Chrome extension

There's an optional Chrome extension that adds Docreview links next to document titles in Google Drive, Documents, and Gmail. It also enables smooth in-page comment navigation in Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides when clicking comment links in Docreview.
